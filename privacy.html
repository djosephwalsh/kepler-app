Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Kepler is made by Daniel Walsh. This policy explains what the Kepler iPhone app collects and why.

Playing without rankings

Kepler works without an account. Your stars, saved flight plans, settings and tutorial progress are stored only on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere.

Optional Game Center rankings

If you connect Game Center, Kepler sends the following to its rankings server:

  • Your Game Center player identifier for this developer, and a signed proof from Apple used to confirm it.
  • Your Game Center display name.
  • Your best delta-v result for each ranked mission.

This is used only to run rankings. Your display name and results are shown publicly on rankings to other players. The server is hosted by Supabase, which may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time for security and operations.

Deleting your rankings data

In the app, open Options and choose Delete online data. This deletes your player record, sessions and scores. The server keeps a one-way hashed marker so a deleted player isn't reconnected without you choosing to. Choosing Disconnect stops further uploads without deleting anything. Rankings data is otherwise kept while the rankings exist.

Tips

Tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Kepler doesn't receive your payment details.

What Kepler doesn't do

Kepler has no advertising or analytics, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't sell or share your data.

Children

Kepler doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above for optional Game Center rankings.

Changes and contact
