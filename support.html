Support

Email djosephwalsh@gmail.com with questions, bugs or ideas. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the mission name if it's about a specific mission.

Where is my progress saved?

Stars, saved flight plans and tutorial progress are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I start over?

From the main menu, open Options and choose Reset all progress. This can't be undone.

How do rankings work?

Rankings are optional and use Game Center. Connect from Options or from a mission result. Your Game Center name and your best delta-v for each ranked mission appear on public rankings.

How do I remove my rankings data?

From the main menu, open Options and choose Delete online data. Your scores are removed from every ranking and Kepler's server forgets your Game Center player. Your progress on the device stays.
